About TaskFlow

Simplifying task management and improving organization.

TaskFlow is a web-based task management application designed to support the organization of activities and responsibilities within the ACG Career Services Office.

Our Purpose

The purpose of TaskFlow is to provide a simple, accessible, and organized environment for managing everyday tasks. The platform helps users monitor progress, prioritize responsibilities, and maintain a clear overview of their workload.

TaskFlow is designed around activities such as career workshops, employer events, student registrations, and administrative responsibilities.

Key Features

Task Management

Create, edit, and delete tasks while keeping responsibilities organized in one place.

Priority Tracking

Assign low, medium, or high priority levels to identify important activities.

Progress Monitoring

Track tasks through pending, in-progress, and completed stages.

Task Analytics

View performance statistics, completion rates, and visual breakdowns of tasks.

Technologies Used

TaskFlow is developed using standard web technologies:

  • HTML5: Provides the structure and semantic content of the website.
  • CSS3: Controls the visual design, responsive layout, and light and dark themes.
  • JavaScript: Implements task management, dynamic content, and analytics calculations.
  • localStorage: Stores tasks and theme preferences within the user's browser.

Academic Project

TaskFlow was developed as a student project for ITC4214 Internet Programming at the American College of Greece.

The project demonstrates practical applications of front-end web development, responsive design, DOM manipulation, event handling, and browser-based data storage.
