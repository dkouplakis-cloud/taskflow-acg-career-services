ABOUT OUR PLATFORM

About TaskFlow

Organize. Collaborate. Achieve.

TaskFlow is a web-based task management platform designed to support the ACG Career Services Office in organizing activities, coordinating responsibilities, and monitoring progress.

Explore TaskFlow

Our Purpose

TaskFlow provides a simple, accessible, and organized environment for managing daily responsibilities. It helps users prioritize activities, track progress, and maintain a clear overview of their workload.

The platform is designed around Career Services activities such as career workshops, employer events, student registrations, and administrative coordination.

What TaskFlow Offers

Explore the features designed to make task management more efficient and organized.

Task Management

Create, edit, search, and delete tasks through an organized interface.

Priority Tracking

Assign low, medium, or high priorities to identify important responsibilities.

Progress Monitoring

Monitor tasks through Pending, In Progress, and Completed stages.

Task Analytics

View completion rates, task statistics, and visual performance breakdowns.

Kanban Board

Organize tasks visually and update their status using drag-and-drop functionality.

Persistent Storage

Save tasks and appearance preferences locally in the browser using localStorage.

OUR ORGANIZATIONAL CONTEXT

ACG Career Services

The Career Services Office at The American College of Greece supports students and alumni in their professional development and career planning.

Through career guidance, workshops, employer engagement, and professional development activities, the office helps students prepare for future opportunities.

TaskFlow is an academic project designed around the organizational needs and activities of this office.

Technologies Used

HTML5

Semantic page structure and accessible website content.

CSS3

Responsive layouts, visual styling, and light and dark themes.

JavaScript

Interactive features, DOM manipulation, event handling, and analytics.

localStorage

Browser-based persistence of tasks and appearance preferences.

Academic Project

TaskFlow was developed for ITC4214 Internet Programming at The American College of Greece.

The project demonstrates front-end web development, responsive design, DOM manipulation, JavaScript event handling, accessibility, and browser-based data storage.

TaskFlow is a student-developed demonstration platform and is not an official ACG system.
